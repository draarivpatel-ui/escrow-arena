      Payoffs are a deterministic integer-cent model, not measurements of a live system. The naive arbiter cannot verify delivery and splits disputed pots 50/50 after its fee; the evidence arbiter rules correctly but is one corruptible principal; the jury assumes honest seats hold the majority and counts seats sharing a funding source as one vote; the timelock trades correctness for a liveness bound. The checksum is an unkeyed SHA-256 tamper-detection digest, not a signature and not authentication. No network request is made.
