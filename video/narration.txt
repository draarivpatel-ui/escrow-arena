EscrowArena is an agentic commerce dispute tribunal and PayPal Orders version two escrow settlement simulator.
When an autonomous buyer agent and a vendor service agent clash over milestone delivery quality, latency breaches, or schema conformance, EscrowArena convenes a four agent arbitration panel.
Players inspect concrete SLA evidence across contract specifications, latency and error telemetry histograms, and signed agent delivery logs.
Clicking Convene Tribunal activates the Buyer Advocate, Seller Counsel, SLA Auditor, and Chief Arbitrator, who compute weighted breach penalties and propose an exact basis point escrow split.
Players can adjust the settlement slider, flag specific breach clauses, and file an official ruling that generates live PayPal Orders version two create, authorize, capture, and refund JSON payloads.
The dual mode settlement ledger tracks every escrow hold and refund alongside a live PayPal Sandbox bearer token verifier.
